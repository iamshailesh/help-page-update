Overview
The
Notification Center
offers a centralized view of real-time alerts generated by Log360 to help you stay aware of configuration issues, system warnings, and other events that may require action. Notifications can be accessed from any page, allowing you to respond promptly when needed.
Click the
icon in the top-right corner of the console to view all active notifications.
Figure 1: Viewing notifications
Filtering notifications
You can filter notifications based on their severity using the drop-down menu in the
Notification Center
. The available options are:
All
Critical
Warning
Info
Figure 2: Filtering notifications
Types of alerts
The product notifies a variety of operational, configuration, and security activities. The notifications displayed may vary based on the events identified in the product. These may include, but are not limited to:
Configuration reminders
Service or component availability issues
Integration or connectivity failures
Disk space or resource-related warnings
Security hardening recommendations
Feature-specific alerts from integrated components
Agent and device status
Managing alerts
The product allows administrators to manage alerts by resolving issues or removing alerts that are no longer needed.
Resolving an alert
Click the
action link
associated with an alert. You will be redirected to the relevant page where you can complete the required configuration or corrective action.
