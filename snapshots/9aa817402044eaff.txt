Overview
The Fortinet reports page presents a structured collection of reports for monitoring events generated by Fortinet devices. These reports cover VPN activity, traffic flow, intrusion attempts, firewall security controls, user authentication, policy management, and system events, enabling administrators to identify threats, investigate access issues, and maintain secure network operations.
