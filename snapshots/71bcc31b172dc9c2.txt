Overview
The Active Directory Reports page provides a centralized set of reports to monitor changes and activities within the Active Directory environment. These reports help track user and group management actions, GPO and policy changes, authentication events, password operations, DNS modifications, and permission updates, enabling administrators to maintain security, meet compliance requirements, and investigate directory-level changes.
