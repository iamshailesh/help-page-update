To configure the Syslog service in your Huawei firewall devices, follow the steps below:
Login to the Huawei firewall device.
Navigate to
System view > Log monitoring > Firewall log stream
To export traffic monitoring logs to EventLog Analyzer server, enter the following details in the space provided:
Info-center loghost <EventLog Analyzer server IP address> 514 facility <facility>
Exit the
configuration
mode.
