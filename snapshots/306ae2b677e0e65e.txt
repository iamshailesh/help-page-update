Search storage (hot storage)
Logs stored here are immediately accessible for real-time search through the live search functionality.
Archival storage (cold storage)
Logs stored here are securely retained to meet long-term compliance requirements and can be retrieved using the
Reload Archive Logs
option when needed for investigations.
For every unit of search storage purchased, you will receive
three times
the
Archival storage

Search storage

Archival storage
at no extra cost.
For organizations that need to retain large volumes of log data for extended periods, an additional storage may be necessary. As a cost-effective solution, you can purchase additional archival storage at
$0.25 per GB
per year.
