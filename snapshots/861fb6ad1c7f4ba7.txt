To configure the Syslog service in your Malwarebytes devices, follow the steps below:
Log into the
Management
console of the Malwarebytes device.
Move to the
Admin
pane and open the
Syslog
Settings
tab.
Click
Change
and tick the
Enable Syslog
check box.
To export traffic monitoring logs to EventLog Analyzer server, enter the following
details in the space provided:
Address <EventLog Analyzer server IP address>
Port <513/514>
Protocol
Payload format <CEF>
Click
OK
to save.
Note
At the moment, only the Malwarebytes Management Console (MBMC) is supported.
