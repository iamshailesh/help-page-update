Overview
The SonicWall reports page provides a comprehensive set of reports to analyze events generated by SonicWall devices across the network. These reports help track allowed and denied traffic, VPN activity, intrusion attempts, user authentication, policy changes, and system health, enabling administrators to identify security risks, investigate incidents, and maintain firewall operations.
