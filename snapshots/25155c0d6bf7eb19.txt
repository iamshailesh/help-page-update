Overview
This page lists the Terminal Server reports available in the product to help monitor gateway access, user connection activity, authorization events, and communication patterns. These reports assist administrators in tracking successful and failed connections, analyzing session behavior, and identifying high-usage users, clients, and resources across terminal server environments.
