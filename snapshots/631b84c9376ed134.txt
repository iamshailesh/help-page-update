Overview
The Meraki reports page offers a structured collection of reports to monitor events generated by Meraki devices. These reports cover traffic flow, user authentication, VPN activity, intrusion attempts, website access, and device severity events, helping administrators detect security threats, analyze usage patterns, and maintain network visibility.
