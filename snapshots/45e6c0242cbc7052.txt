Overview
This page explains how to view and manage compliance reports. You can view comprehensive audit data, export reports, and analyze device and user activity for detailed compliance insights.
Steps to view and manage compliance reports
Log in to your Log360 account.
Navigate to the
Compliance
tab.
Under the configured compliance mandates, click
View Reports
for the compliance standard you want to review.
Figure 1: Viewing reports
The reports page displays a list of categories with detailed audit reports.
Figure 2: Viewing reports
To view detailed audit reports, click
Comprehensive Audit Reports
at the top of the page.
Figure 3: Viewing Comprehensive Audit Reports
To export reports for the selected compliance mandate, click Export As in the top-right corner.
Figure 4: Exporting reports
In the export window, use the
Export drop-down
to choose the reports you want to export:
All Reports
All Overview Reports
Currently Viewed Reports
Select Reports
Figure 5: Exporting reports
From the
Export As drop-down
, choose the file format (
PDF
or
CSV
) and then click Export to download the reports.
