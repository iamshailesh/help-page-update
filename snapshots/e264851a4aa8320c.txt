Overview
The Cisco reports page provides a structured set of reports to monitor and analyze events generated by Cisco devices across the network. These reports cover areas such as network risk, traffic flow, VPN activity, firewall events, intrusion detection, system status, and configuration changes, helping administrators detect threats, track user and device activity, and maintain network stability.
