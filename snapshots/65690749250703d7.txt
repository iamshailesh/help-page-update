Overview
The Windows reports page provides a consolidated view of security, system, and operational events collected from Windows-based devices. These reports are grouped into logical categories such as logon activity, system events, threat detection, device auditing, policy enforcement, and application activity, enabling administrators to monitor user behavior, detect suspicious activities, and assess the overall security posture of the environment.
