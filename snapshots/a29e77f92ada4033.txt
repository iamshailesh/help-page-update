Storage usage
Below the component rows, the License page shows your two storage allocations:
Search Storage
is hot storage. Logs here are fully indexed and immediately searchable for investigation, alerting, and correlation. The Purchased column shows both the base allocation included in your plan and any storage add-ons purchased separately (for example,
800 GB (700 GB included + 100 GB add-on
)). Retention period is shown in parentheses next to the label (for example,
Retention Period: 90 Days
) and is configurable via Storage Tiers.
Archival Storage
is cold storage. Logs here are compressed and retained for long-term compliance purposes. Archived logs are not directly searchable; they must be reloaded into Search Storage first via
Settings â Data Storage â Reload Archive Logs
. Retention period is fixed at the plan level (for example,
Retention Period: 10 Years
) and is shown next to the label.

Stop Log Collection
â Halts log ingestion entirely when the storage limit is reached. Existing data is preserved and administrators are notified. Recommended for compliance environments where incomplete log coverage is unacceptable.
