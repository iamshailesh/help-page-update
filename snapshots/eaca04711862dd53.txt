Overview
This page outlines the various Forcepoint firewall reports supported by the product, enabling administrators to monitor firewall events, analyze traffic patterns, track user logon activities, and gain visibility into web, email, and intrusion-related security incidents. These reports also help assess system-level changes and device severity states, supporting effective security monitoring and troubleshooting.
