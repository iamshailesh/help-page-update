Overview
This page lists the Sysmon-related reports available in the product that help monitor low-level system activities on endpoints. These reports capture detailed telemetry on process execution, registry and file operations, network activity, WMI events, driver loading, and configuration changes, enabling deep forensic analysis and proactive threat detection.
