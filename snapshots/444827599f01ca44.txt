Overview
The Global Search feature in ManageEngine Log360 allows you to search across all major sections of the product console, such as
Reports, Compliance, Search, Security, Alerts,
and
Settings
. It is accessible from all tabs of the product console, enabling quick navigation to specific features, report categories, configuration pages, or compliance standards, improving the efficiency and productivity of SOC teams. Global Search displays tab-specific content, allowing you to quickly view information from the respective sections without navigating between tabs.

Using Global Search
To open
Global Search
, click the
search bar
at the top of the product console or use the keyboard shortcut
Ctrl + Space
.
Figure 2: Navigating to global search
When opened,
Global Search
displays results and suggestions organized into the following sections:
Recent Searches
This section lists your most recent queries, allowing you to revisit reports, settings, or configurations you accessed earlier.
Frequent Searches
This section shows the most commonly searched items across the product console. It highlights popular reports and configurations used by administrators and analysts.
What's New in Log360
This section highlights newly added features in the product console. Clicking a feature takes you directly to its corresponding page in the product console.
