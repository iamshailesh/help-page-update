Log360 Cloud allows you to export the results of a search in the form of a report. To export a search result, follow the steps given below.
Once you have obtained the result for a search, you will see the option
Export As
.
You can choose to export the report either as a
PDF
file or a
CSV
file. Click on the required file format to export the search result.
Now the search results will be compiled in the form of a downloadable report.
Now, click on the
icon next to the
Export As
button. Then click on
Download
, which will appear when you hover over the required search result.
The report will now be downloaded to your default download location.
