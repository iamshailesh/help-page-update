Overview
The Palo Alto reports page provides a comprehensive set of reports to monitor events generated by Palo Alto devices. These reports cover firewall security detections, allowed and denied traffic, intrusion attempts, user authentication, VPN activity, and system events, enabling administrators to identify threats, investigate access issues, and maintain secure firewall operations.
