Overview:
The Device Summary is Log360 Cloud's analytical console to view the overall device summary events and contextual data. Users can find the machine summary and event summary for the selected period, device severity events, and alerts summary.
Access:
Users can access the Device Summary from multiple dashboards of Log360 Cloud such as Reports, Log Source, Alerts, Incidents and others.
Note:
Guest role members have no access to Device Summary.
