The MSSP edition of Log360 Cloud enables Managed Security Service Providers to deploy Log360 Cloud to monitor their Customers across geographical locations. This edition comes with a centralized dashboard to manage Customers as well as separate dashboards for different Customers.
A few highlights of the Log360 Cloud MSSP edition include:
Centralizes log management
Supports multiple Customers across different geographical locations
Ensures secure data storage and transmission
Exclusive segmented and secured view for various Customers of the MSSP.
FAQs
Who can use the MSSP edition?
The MSSP edition can be used by Managed Security Service Providers to provide SIEM service for upto 50 Customers.
Where are the collected logs stored?
The collected logs are stored on the cloud in a data center as specified by your Location.
