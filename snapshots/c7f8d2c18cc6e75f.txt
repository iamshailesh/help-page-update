Steps to rebrand the Log360 client
Overview
Log360 allows you to customize the product interface by replacing default logos, images, and links with your own branding. This feature is particularly useful for Managed Security Service Providers (MSSPs) who want to deliver a personalized experience for their clients.
You can modify the logos and images used in the product interface and reports, as well as the strings and links displayed on login pages and other sections of the product.
This page explains how you can rebrand the Log360 client to reflect your organizationâs branding.

To update, locate the string or link you want to customize.
Enter the new string or link in the corresponding field.
Click

NOTE
You can customize all ZohoCorp/ManageEngine images and links as per your requirement
