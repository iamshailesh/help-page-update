Overview
The Sophos reports page provides a centralized set of reports to analyze events generated by Sophos devices and endpoints. These reports cover VPN activity, traffic flow, intrusion attempts, firewall security controls, user authentication, rule management, endpoint health, and system events, enabling administrators to detect threats, investigate access issues, and maintain secure network operations.
