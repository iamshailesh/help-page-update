Centralised Technician audit
The Centralized Technician Audit provides a unified view of audit data for all users, including MSSP admins, operators, and customer portal users. It enables you to track and review the actions of MSSP admins and operators performed on customer accounts in a single location.
Each audit log contains the following details:
Technician's name
Customer on which the action was performed
Time of the action
The module in which the action occurred (e.g., Alerts, Compliance, etc.)
The message generated during the operation
Note:
MSSP super admin and all other admins
can view audit logs for all Customers and technicians while
MSSP operators
can view audit logs for their assigned Customers.
Accessing the Technician Audit Dashboard
Go to
Settings
Navigate to
Admin-> Admin Settings-
>
Technician Audit
.
Adding or removing columns:
Click the
Add or Remove Columns
button in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
In the
Select Columns
dialog box, choose the columns you want to add or remove.
To rearrange the columns, click and drag them from the right side of the dialog box.
Searching for information:
Click the
Search
button on the top-left corner of the dashboard.
Once enabled, the columns in the dashboard becomes searchable .
Auditing Specific Technicians or Customers
Auditing technicians:
Click the
Select Technician
button at the top of the dashboard.
In the dialog box, choose the technicians or usernames you want to audit.
Auditing customers:
Click the
Select Customers
dropdown at the top of the dashboard.
Select the customers whose actions you want to audit.
Note:
The MSSP admin and operator 's actions will be audited with the customer name as "Log360 Cloud MSSP".
