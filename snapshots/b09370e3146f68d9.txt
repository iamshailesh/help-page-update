MSSP edition
The MSSP edition is designed for managed security service providers operating multiple customer tenants from a single portal. It uses the same component-based model as Log360 Cloud, but pricing is slightly higher to account for multi-tenancy capabilities. Each tenant is licensed independently using the same component and edition structure.
MSSP subscriptions can be billed yearly or monthly. Monthly billing is exclusive to the MSSP edition.
For full details on managing tenants and MSSP-specific configuration, see the MSSP Edition section of this documentation.
Free trial
Log360 Cloud offers a 30-day free trial with full Enterprise access and no credit card required. Trial accounts can be upgraded to a paid subscription at any time from the License page.
