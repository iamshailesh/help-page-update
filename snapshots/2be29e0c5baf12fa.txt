To estimate the sizing requirements for log storage,
Navigate to
Admin
under
Settings
tab.
Click on
License
under
General
.
Click on
Storage Estimator
button on the top right corner.
You will see
Collecting data please wait
... as shown below.
As the loader keeps loading, inputs for autofill such as number of configured devices, estimated logs count, etc. are retrieved by Log360 Cloud.
Events count for one prior week is obtained for autofill. In case the same is not available, autofill will not be done.
On the top of the storage estimator landing page, Log360 Cloud will display a note if autofill is not done.
In this case, you have to fill in all relevant fields manually.
In case of successful autofill, the fields will be filled automatically.
Click
Calculate
.
On the right side,
Estimated Storage
will be displayed.
